PLACEHOLDER - review before the first store submission.

Eventra helps you discover concerts, festivals, meetups, comedy nights and
food fairs near you, book tickets in a few taps and keep them on your phone.

- A smart feed of upcoming events, with filters for date, price, distance
  and category
- A full-screen map of what is on nearby
- Secure checkout with a rotating QR ticket that works offline
- Reminders and add-to-calendar
- Reviews from people who went
- Organizer mode: create events, track sales and check guests in with QR
- English, French and Arabic (right-to-left), light and dark themes
